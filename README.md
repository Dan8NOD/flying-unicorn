# Pink Unicorn
A small browser shooter: fly a pink unicorn, collect gold rings, hearts and shield stars, dodge storm clouds, bats and crystal spikes, and beat the flying-saucer boss in each of 8 chapters. A mermaid sidekick follows and helps in boss fights. Rainbow-curtain transitions between chapters (alternating sweep direction, next chapter named at full cover) and a short animated ending with sunrise, rainbow and fireworks.

Chapters: Candy Clouds, Mermaid Bay, Starry Night, Aurora Peaks, Rainbow Canyon, Coral Reef, Crystal Caves, Storm Fortress. Each chapter has its own run-up time and spawn pace, so later chapters run longer and denser.

Source only, no build step, no assets (all art and sound is drawn/synthesised in code).
Run: open `index.html`, or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`). Works with mouse, touch (iPad) and keyboard. M = mute, P = pause.
