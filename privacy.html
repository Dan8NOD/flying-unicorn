Privacy Policy

Pink Unicorn (Pink Winged Unicorn) · Effective November 2, 2026

The Pink Unicorn app collects no data. We don't ask for, collect, store or share personal information from anyone, children or adults.

The App Store and Google Play app

  • No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases. The Sparkle Shop uses rings collected in the game, never real money.
  • Your progress (chapters, stars, Golden Horseshoes, rings, unicorn looks) and your settings (sound, vibration, easy mode) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • All art, music and voices come with the app. It works fully offline and makes no network requests while you play.
  • Game controllers, if you connect one, are read only to steer the unicorn.

Children

Pink Unicorn is made for all ages, including young children. Because we collect nothing, there is no personal information to review or delete.

Website and the "2 Devices" mode

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use cookies or analytics.

Only on the website, the optional "2 Devices" mode connects two players' browsers directly to each other (WebRTC). To find a route between the two devices, the browsers contact a public Google STUN server, which sees their IP addresses, and the two devices see each other's network addresses. No game data goes to us. This mode is not in the App Store app.
