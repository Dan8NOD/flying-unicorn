How to play

How do I fly?
Drag anywhere on the screen and the unicorn follows your finger. She throws pony-dust stars by herself.
How do I dash?
Double-tap. Dashing makes you safe for a moment, so use it to slip through danger.
What is Rainbow Nova?
Collect sparkles to fill the Sparkle Meter. When it's full, tap the meter to clear the screen.
Can two people play?
Yes. Choose 2 Players · one screen. Each player steers on their own half of the screen, or connect two game controllers.
Is there an easier mode?
Yes. Tap Easy mode at the bottom left of the title screen for extra lives. If you lose, you can try the same chapter again right away.
How do I replay a chapter?
Open the map from the title screen and tap any island you have reached.

Common questions

I don't hear any sound.
Check that the device isn't on silent and its volume is up. Then tap the gear at the top right of the title screen and check Music, Sound effects and Voices.
Can I use a game controller?
Yes. Any controller your device supports works: the stick steers and B dashes. A second controller steers the mermaid in 2-player mode.
Do I need internet?
No. The app works fully offline.
Where is my progress saved?
Only on this device. Deleting the app deletes it.
Is any information collected?
No. See our privacy policy.
